# 🎮 3D Scrabble Escape Room

Welcome to the most **INSANE** 3D escape room experience ever created! This is a fully immersive 3D world where you solve Scrabble puzzles in a breathtaking 3D environment.

## 🌟 **What Makes This INSANE:**

### 🎯 **Full 3D Environment**
- **Real 3D Rooms** - Walk through actual 3D spaces with walls, floors, and ceilings
- **Interactive 3D Objects** - Click and manipulate 3D Scrabble tiles, doors, and puzzles
- **Atmospheric Lighting** - Dynamic lighting that changes based on your progress
- **Particle Systems** - Thousands of floating particles that match each room's theme
- **Realistic Physics** - Objects cast shadows and respond to lighting

### 🎨 **Visual Effects**
- **Post-Processing** - Bloom, chromatic aberration, and depth of field effects
- **Floating Animations** - Tiles float and rotate in 3D space
- **Glow Effects** - Objects emit light and glow when selected
- **Fog and Atmosphere** - Atmospheric fog that changes per room theme
- **Crystal Shards** - Floating crystal particles that react to your actions

### 🎮 **Interactive Elements**
- **3D Scrabble Tiles** - Click tiles that float in 3D space

- **Holographic Displays** - Sci-fi style floating text displays

### 🎵 **Immersive Audio**
- **3D Spatial Audio** - Sound comes from specific 3D locations
- **Atmospheric Sounds** - Room-specific ambient audio
- **Interactive Sound Effects** - Different sounds for different actions
- **Sound Visualization** - Visual bars that react to audio
